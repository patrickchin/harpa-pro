<GuideStep
  number={1}
  title="Open Members"
  screenshot="members-team"
  screenshotAlt="Project Members screen with role filters and Add member action"
>
  Open the project and tap **Members**. Only the project Owner can add or
  remove members.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={2} title="Invite by email">
  Tap **Add member**, enter the email the teammate uses for Harpa Pro, choose a
  role, and tap **Send invite**.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={3} title="Choose the right access">
  **Owner** controls the project and membership. **Editor** creates and edits
  reports. **Viewer** reads project and report information without changing
  it. A project has one Owner; invitations add Editors or Viewers.
</GuideStep>
