## Edit a draft report

1. Open the draft and select **Report**.
2. Find the card that needs a correction.
3. Tap the pencil action on that card.
4. Update the fields in the edit screen.
5. Tap **Save** to return to the report.

Pencil actions are available for report details, weather, workers, materials,
issues, next steps, and additional sections. This keeps each edit focused on
the part of the report you are reviewing.

## Add or place photos

Photo groups can be placed under a report section or issue. Use the placement
control on an unplaced photo group, choose its destination, and save. You can
also use a card's attachment action to select an existing report photo.

## Edit a finalized report

Finalized reports are read-only. To make a correction:

1. Open **Report Actions**.
2. Tap **Unfinalize report** and confirm.
3. Open the returned draft.
4. Use a card's pencil action, save the correction, and review the report.
5. Finalize it again when it is ready.

Owners and Editors can unfinalize. Viewers do not see that action.

## Good to know

> Editing generated content changes the report, not the original source note.
> If the source itself is wrong, correct or remove the note and let Harpa Pro
> update the draft.

Do not start a regeneration while a manual edit is saving. The app disables
conflicting actions during that short window.

## Troubleshooting

- **Pencil actions are missing:** The report may be finalized or your role may
  be read-only.
- **A saved edit reverts:** Wait for **Saving…** to finish before leaving the
  report, then refresh once.
- **A photo appears in the wrong section:** Open its placement control and
  choose a different destination.
- **Unfinalize is unavailable:** Ask an Owner or Editor on the project to return
  the report to draft.
