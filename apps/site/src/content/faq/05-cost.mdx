---
question: "What does Harpa Pro cost?"
order: 5
---

Harpa Pro is free to download. The Free plan includes 1 million weighted input
tokens, 100,000 weighted output tokens, and uploads up to 5 MB per file each UTC
calendar month. Voice notes remain available on Free, and there is no
report-count limit.

Pro raises those allowances to 10 million weighted input tokens, 1 million
weighted output tokens, and 50 MB per file. Pro is offered as monthly and
annual auto-renewing subscriptions. The App Store or Play Store displays the
localized price before you subscribe. There is no free trial.

Cancel from **Account Details → Manage subscription**. Pro remains active
through the end of the paid period. **Restore purchases** reconnects an
eligible store purchase after reinstalling or changing devices. Deleting a
Harpa account does not cancel the separate store subscription.
