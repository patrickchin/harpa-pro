<GuideStep number={1} title="Open your profile">
  From **Projects**, open the profile action. Tap the user card to review or
  edit your name and company details.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep
  number={2}
  title="Review usage"
  screenshot="usage"
  screenshotAlt="Harpa Pro Usage History with monthly totals and recent activity"
>
  Tap **Usage This Month** to see the limits, current totals, recent AI
  activity, and reset timing available for the account. Free includes 1 million
  weighted input tokens, 100,000 weighted output tokens, and files up to 5 MB.
  Pro includes 10 million weighted input tokens, 1 million weighted output
  tokens, and files up to 50 MB. Allowances reset at the start of each UTC
  calendar month. Voice notes remain available on Free, and there is no
  report-count limit.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={3} title="Manage billing">
  In **Account Details**, use **Manage subscription** to review or cancel an
  active store subscription. Use **Restore purchases** after reinstalling or
  changing devices. Pro is available as monthly and annual subscriptions, and
  the App Store or Play Store shows the localized price before purchase.
  Cancelling keeps Pro active through the end of the paid period.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={4} title="Request account deletion">
  Open Account Details, tap **Delete account**, review the preview, and confirm
  with your account email. Deleting Harpa does not cancel an App Store or Play
  Store subscription; use **Manage subscription** first if you also want to
  cancel. If you cannot access the app, use the [account-deletion
  page](/account-deletion).
</GuideStep>

Use **Clear cached data** if a screen remains stale after refreshing, or use
**Sign Out** to remove the active session from the device. Neither action
deletes server records.
