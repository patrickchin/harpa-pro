<GuideStep
  number={1}
  title="Open a finalized report"
  screenshot="reports-list"
  screenshotAlt="Harpa Pro Reports list showing draft and finalized reports"
>
  Open the project, tap **Reports**, and choose a report from the finalized
  section. Draft reports must be reviewed and finalized first.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={2} title="Open the PDF preview">
  Open **Report Actions** and tap **View PDF**. Harpa Pro builds the PDF on the
  device and opens the preview.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep
  number={3}
  title="Review the PDF"
  screenshot="pdf-preview"
  screenshotAlt="Finalized Harpa Pro report displayed in the PDF preview"
>
  Scan the summary, figures, issues, people, materials, and actions. Close the
  preview if the report needs a correction.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={4} title="Share or save it">
  Tap **Share PDF** in the preview, then choose a destination from the iPhone
  share sheet. You can also use **Save PDF** from Report Actions.
</GuideStep>

If a correction is needed, return to the report, unfinalize it if authorized,
edit the affected card, and finalize it again.
