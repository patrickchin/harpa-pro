## Open the member list

Open a project and tap **Members**. The list shows each teammate's name, email,
and project role. Use the role filters to focus on Owners, Editors, or Viewers.

## Invite a teammate

Only the project Owner can manage members.

1. Tap **Add member**.
2. Enter the teammate's email address.
3. Choose **Editor** or **Viewer**.
4. Tap **Send invite**.

Use the same email address the teammate uses for Harpa Pro. The project appears
in their Projects list after access is active and their app refreshes.

## Choose a role

- **Owner:** Controls project details and membership, creates and edits reports,
  and can delete the project. A project has one Owner.
- **Editor:** Contributes to the project, creates reports, edits drafts, and can
  unfinalize reports.
- **Viewer:** Reads project and report information without changing it.

The Owner is calculated from project ownership and is not an invite option.

## Remove a member

On the Members screen, the Owner can tap the remove action beside an Editor or
Viewer and confirm. The Owner cannot remove their own ownership row from this
screen.

## Good to know

> Membership belongs to one project. Inviting someone to one site does not
> automatically grant access to other projects.

Email addresses are normalized before an invitation is sent. If a teammate is
already present, refresh the list rather than sending repeated invitations.

## Troubleshooting

- **Add member is missing:** Confirm that your role is Owner.
- **The teammate cannot see the project:** Confirm the invited email, then ask
  them to pull down on Projects to refresh.
- **An invitation reports an error:** Correct the email if needed and retry
  after the error clears.
- **You cannot remove someone:** Only the Owner can remove an Editor or Viewer.
