The useful comparison starts with the work each buying route leaves you
to manage. Finding a product is one part of an order. You also need an
agreed specification, a payment route, quality checks, export handling,
and delivery to the destination.

A factory may sell through Alibaba.com, 1688, and its own sales team.
Trading companies can also use marketplaces. The channel you choose and
the business supplying your goods are separate things to verify.

## Alibaba.com provides a route for international sourcing

Alibaba.com lets buyers search suppliers, send requests for quotation,
discuss customisation, and place orders. Its buyer guidance describes
samples, detailed purchase orders, and inspection as parts of sourcing.
[Alibaba.com's sourcing process](https://activity.alibaba.com/page/HowItWorks/Page.html)

For an architect comparing door hardware or furniture, the platform can
be a starting point for gathering offers against one buying brief. Ask
each seller the same questions about material, quantity, packaging,
inspection access, and the named delivery point.

Check the legal entity behind the profile. Open available assessment
reports and establish whether the seller manufactures your product,
sources it elsewhere, or does both. A large catalogue needs the same
order-specific checks as a short one.

If you plan to use Trade Assurance, confirm the particular order's
eligibility and complete the supported payment process. Write product
requirements and shipment terms into the online order. Alibaba.com
describes protection in relation to eligible transactions and agreed
terms; a refund depends on the applicable policy and evidence.
[Alibaba.com: Trade Assurance coverage and records](https://seller.alibaba.com/businessblogs/alibaba-trade-assurance-how-order-protection-works-for-buyers-and-sellers-px002dpu1)

Do not assume that a conversation with an Alibaba.com supplier means a
separate transfer or off-platform order receives the same protection.
Read the instructions shown for the order you intend to pay.

## 1688 needs a clear plan for receiving and exporting

Alibaba Group describes 1688 as a domestic wholesale marketplace in China.
That makes the scope of an individual offer important for an overseas
buyer. Confirm exactly where the seller will deliver and what export work,
if any, it agrees to perform.
[Alibaba Group: 1688](https://home.alibabagroup.com/en-US/about-alibaba-businesses-1941299332078632960)

A domestic warehouse price can be useful when you already have someone
receiving goods in China. That person or business may also need to check
deliveries, coordinate packing, resolve problems, and arrange export.
Agree those responsibilities and fees before ordering.

Overseas payment options exist. WorldFirst describes World Pay as an
integration for paying 1688 orders from a WorldFirst balance. Check whether
your business and location qualify, which account checks apply, and the
fees shown in the current service terms.
[WorldFirst: buying from China with 1688 and World Pay](https://www.worldfirst.com/global/product/pay/1688-world-pay/)

Payment access does not answer the rest of the procurement questions.
Confirm communication support, technical documents, returns arrangements,
inspection access, and the delivery address for each seller. An agent's
promise to handle these tasks should have a written scope.

## Direct purchasing puts more coordination in the contract

You may reach a factory through a trade fair, a referral, its own website,
or an existing project contact. Buying directly can suit a repeat product
or an order that needs sustained technical discussion.

Establish the contracting entity, production location, and who handles
export. Then agree the commercial and quality terms. Direct contact
gives you an opportunity to clarify the work; it does not establish that
the price is lowest or that the factory can meet every requirement.

For custom joinery, ask who develops shop drawings, approves dimensions,
sources hardware, and signs off the finish. Name the person authorised to
approve changes on your side. Changes sent casually through several
contacts can leave the factory working to an outdated drawing.

Check how disputes, rework, and payment milestones will be handled. Seek
appropriate professional help for a substantial contract. Keep the agreed
documents and approvals together throughout production.

## Compare the routes against the same order

| Question | Alibaba.com | 1688 | Direct factory |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who is selling? | Verify the profile's contracting entity | Verify the seller's contracting entity | Verify the factory and contracting entity |
| Where does delivery end? | Confirm the quotation's named place | Confirm domestic or export delivery scope | Negotiate and document the named place |
| How is payment made? | Check the order's supported process | Check available payment route and eligibility | Agree a verified banking or provider route |
| Who checks quality? | Arrange the agreed inspection scope | Arrange receipt and production checks as needed | Agree access and acceptance checks |
| What is the comparison price? | Complete landed order cost | Goods plus coordination and export costs | Complete landed order cost |

These are questions to resolve, rather than fixed promises about any
channel. The individual supplier and agreement determine the details.

## Test a comparable product before choosing

Take one item from your materials schedule and send an identical brief
through the routes you are considering. Include quantity, required finish,
packaging, destination, and evidence requirements. Ask for sample terms
and the point at which production lead time begins.

Compare the complete cost and the unresolved work. If one offer omits
inspection, domestic delivery, or export packaging, add a properly sourced
allowance before comparing it with another offer. A headline listing price
does not establish the final specification or total spend.

Order a sample from the shortlisted supplier and check how accurately the
seller follows instructions. Confirm destination requirements before a
larger commitment, using the [main buying guide](/procurement/how-to-buy-from-china)
and your broker's current advice.

Choose the route whose responsibilities you can manage and whose complete
offer meets the project. Save that decision with the quotation, sample
approval, and written order so the next purchase starts from a clear record.
