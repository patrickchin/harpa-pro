<GuideStep number={1} title="Open your profile">
  From **Projects**, open the profile action. Tap the user card to review or
  edit your name and company details.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep
  number={2}
  title="Review usage"
  screenshot="usage"
  screenshotAlt="Harpa Pro Usage History with monthly totals and recent activity"
>
  Tap **Usage This Month** to see the limits, current totals, recent AI
  activity, and reset timing available for the account.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={3} title="Clear local data or sign out">
  Use **Clear cached data** if an open screen remains stale after refreshing.
  This does not delete server records. Use **Sign Out** to remove the active
  session from the device.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={4} title="Request account deletion">
  Open Account Details, tap **Delete account**, review the preview, and confirm
  with your account email. If you cannot access the app, use the
  [account-deletion page](/account-deletion).
</GuideStep>
