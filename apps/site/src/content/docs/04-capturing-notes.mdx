## Add a text note

1. Open a draft report and select **Notes**.
2. Tap **Type a site note…** and enter the update.
3. Tap **Send**.

Keep each note focused on one event: completed work, labour, materials,
weather, an issue, or the next action. Specific notes produce a clearer report.

## Record a voice note

1. Tap **Record** in the note composer.
2. Speak a short field update.
3. Tap the send control to stop and upload the recording, or cancel to discard
   it.
4. Wait while Harpa Pro uploads, transcribes, and processes the note.

The timeline shows progress and offers retry actions if the upload or voice
processing fails.

## Add photos

Tap **Photo** to open the camera, or tap the attachment button and choose
**Photo library**.

In the camera you can take up to 20 photos in one session. Review the thumbnail
strip, remove unwanted shots, and tap **Done**. The group stays together as one
batch note. Photos can later be placed under a report section or issue.

## Work with document notes

Existing document notes remain visible in a report's Notes view and open with
the device handler. Adding a new document is not exposed in the current iPhone
composer; use text, voice, camera, or the photo library for new captures.

## Edit or remove a note

Open a note's options menu to use the actions available for that note. Removing
a source note can mark an existing draft report for an update.

## Good to know

> After the first report has been generated, changes to source notes mark the
> draft as needing an update. Harpa Pro normally starts that update
> automatically while the draft is open.

Camera shots can optionally be saved to the camera roll. If photo access is
off, Harpa Pro explains how to open system Settings.

## Troubleshooting

- **Voice processing fails:** Keep the draft open and tap the retry action on
  the voice note. Check your connection before recording again.
- **A photo is still uploading:** Wait for its progress indicator to finish.
  Use retry if the upload reports an error.
- **The camera is unavailable:** Allow camera access in iPhone Settings and
  return to the report.
- **A note is missing from the report:** Open **Report**, wait for the automatic
  update, or use the regenerate action to retry.
