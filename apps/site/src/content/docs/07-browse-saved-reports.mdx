<GuideStep
  number={1}
  title="Open Reports"
  screenshot="reports-list"
  screenshotAlt="Project Reports list with draft and finalized report sections"
>
  Open a project and tap **Reports**. Pull down to refresh if a teammate has
  just created or changed a report.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={2} title="Check the report state">
  **Drafts** can accept notes, generation, and edits. **Finalized** reports are
  read-only records ready for PDF preview or sharing.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={3} title="Reopen the report">
  Tap a draft to continue working, or tap a finalized row to review the saved
  report. Report Actions contains the options available for that state and
  your project role.
</GuideStep>
