<GuideStep number={1} title="Create a project">
  On **Projects**, tap **Add project**. Enter a project name, add an address or
  client when useful, then tap **Create project**.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep
  number={2}
  title="Open the project"
  screenshot="projects-list"
  screenshotAlt="Harpa Pro Projects list showing available construction projects"
>
  Tap a project row to open its overview. From there you can open Reports,
  Members, and project details.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={3} title="Edit the details">
  On the project overview, tap the pencil action. Change the project fields and
  tap **Save changes**. Owners and Editors can edit; Viewers are read-only.
</GuideStep>

<GuideStep number={4} title="Delete only when intended">
  Owners can open **Edit project**, tap **Delete project**, and confirm the
  warning. Deletion is permanent, so export any records you must keep first.
</GuideStep>
