## Review what will happen

Account deletion is permanent. Before confirming, Harpa Pro shows a preview
covering:

- solo projects that will be deleted;
- shared projects whose ownership will transfer;
- shared projects you will leave; and
- personal file rows that will be removed.

Shared project records may remain available to other members. Export any
reports you need before deleting the account.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open **Profile** and tap the user card.
2. Scroll to the account-deletion warning on **Account Details**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Review the deletion preview.
5. Type your account email in the confirmation field.
6. Tap **Delete account** again.

Harpa Pro signs out active devices after deletion completes.

## Use the web request page

If you cannot access the app, open
[harpapro.com/account-deletion](/account-deletion). The page explains the
request path and provides the support email. Send the request from the account
email when possible so ownership can be verified.

## Get help

Email [patrick@harpapro.com](mailto:patrick@harpapro.com) with:

- the screen and project where the problem occurred;
- the action you expected to complete;
- the exact error message; and
- the app version shown at the bottom of Profile.

Do not include confidential site notes, verification codes, or customer files
unless support specifically asks for a safe example.

## Good to know

> Deleting the app from an iPhone does not delete the Harpa Pro account. Use
> Account Details or the public request page for account deletion.

Account deletion is different from deleting a project. Project deletion
removes one project and its reports; account deletion removes the identity and
applies the previewed ownership rules across projects.

## Troubleshooting

- **The confirmation button is disabled:** Enter the complete account email
  shown on Account Details and wait for the preview to load.
- **The preview cannot load:** Cancel, confirm your connection, and open the
  deletion dialog again.
- **Deletion fails after confirmation:** Keep the account signed in and send
  the displayed error to support.
- **You no longer control the email:** Use the public request page and explain
  the access problem so support can verify the request safely.
