## Update account details

1. Open the profile action from the app header.
2. Tap the user card at the top of **Profile**.
3. On **Account Details**, tap **Edit**.
4. Change your full name or company name.
5. Tap **Save**.

Your sign-in email is read-only on this screen because it identifies the
account. Contact support if access must move to another address.

## Review usage and limits

From Profile, tap **Usage This Month**. **Usage History** shows the limits that
apply to the account, current-month totals, activity by model, recent AI
events, and monthly breakdowns when data is available.

Usage-limit dialogs use the same counters. They show the action that reached a
limit, the current usage, and its reset time.

## Open the privacy policy

Tap **Privacy Policy** on Profile to open the current hosted notice. The public
policy is also available at [harpapro.com/privacy](/privacy).

## Clear cached data

If reports or voice notes look stale or out of place:

1. Tap **Clear cached data** on Profile.
2. Review the warning.
3. Tap **Clear cache**.

This clears the in-memory query cache and refetches open screens. It does not
delete projects, reports, notes, or account data.

## Sign out

Tap **Sign Out** at the bottom of Profile. Harpa Pro removes the active session
from the device and returns to email sign-in.

## Good to know

> Pull down on Profile or Usage History to refresh information before assuming
> a limit or profile change is missing.

The notification row is informational in the current release. Developer tools
appear only in development or fixture-enabled builds.

## Troubleshooting

- **Profile changes do not save:** Keep the editor open, check the inline
  error, and retry with a connection.
- **Usage is empty:** Generate or process a report first, then refresh Usage
  History. Limits can still appear before event history exists.
- **A screen still looks stale after refresh:** Use **Clear cached data** once.
- **Sign-out does not complete:** Close and reopen the app, then confirm whether
  email sign-in is shown.
